# AriNav: finals pitch (short version)

3 minutes, one line per slide of [`final-pitch.html`](final-pitch.html). The slides carry the detail: say the line, let the picture land, move on.
About 250 spoken words plus the 75 s demo.

---

**1 · Hook** *(walker on the route)* · 10 s
> Where is the 3D printer? The plan is a PDF, the machine list is a spreadsheet, and the person who knows went home. With AriNav, you just ask the building.

**2 · Find. Route. Edit. React.** *(four phone screens)* · 12 s
> Four things, one conversation, by voice or text. It finds, it routes, it changes the building when you confirm, and it reacts when something closes.

**3 · Live demo** *(switch to the app)* · 75 s
1. "Where is the 3D printer?" → *"It asks which one instead of guessing."*
2. "I'm in Office 2.04. Take me to the CNC mill, no stairs." → let the route and voice play.
3. "The office lift is closed." → **Confirm** → *"No step-free way left, and it tells you."*
4. "Add a door between 2.04 and 2.05." → **Confirm**. ⚠ On 25 Oct the agent answered "I can't add or edit doors". Rehearse this, or say "Close the door between Office 2.04 and the corridor" instead (that one shows the Confirm card, as on slide 2).
5. Developer: drag a machine → **Save** → *"Machines move. The map keeps up."*

Stalls for more than 5 s: switch to the backup video at the same step.

**4 · Does it work** *(rings fill)* · 25 s
> 650 of 655 benchmark questions, end to end. 99 percent of all 2,785, with Claude Haiku, in 17 minutes, for eight dollars. All 240 buildings ingested. Every route is exact to the centimetre. Sunday's building takes one command.

**5 · How it works** *(plan → graph → 3D → talk)* · 20 s
> Floor plan in, conversation out. Routing is deterministic, so it gives the same answer every time. Claude does the talking and asks before it changes anything. Opus checks every step of the pipeline.

**6 · Built for people** *(gallery)* · 15 s
> We also built walk-along voice guidance, live closures, a phone view, a developer mode, and a one-click high-quality 3D model. And we tested it on tricky users.

**7 · Close** *(building)* · 5 s
> Any building. A map you can talk to. Thank you.
